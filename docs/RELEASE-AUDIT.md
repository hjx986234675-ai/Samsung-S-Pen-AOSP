## Distribution conclusion

No Samsung redistribution permission has been obtained. Do not publicly distribute complete modified APKs/modules. Noncommercial use is not a substitute for permission. Publishing independently authored adapters and interoperability notes avoids directly distributing the original application payload, but is not a zero-risk legal certification. Exact donor licenses and applicable law still require individual assessment.

Sources: [Chinese Software Protection Regulations](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2013-02/08/c_126468744.htm), Articles 8 (copying, distribution including gifts, network dissemination), 16 (necessary local modification and restrictions on providing modified copies to others), 17 (study/research); [Samsung China service terms](https://terms.account.samsung.com/contents/legal/chn/zho/general.html) and [mobile software terms](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/legal/LGL10000282/) describe restrictions on reverse engineering, modification and derivative works. These web terms do not establish the exact license supplied with the SM-T970 donor.

[XDA forum rules](https://xdaforums.com/t/xda-developers-forum-rules.4200559/latest) prohibit warez and set posting requirements. This does not grant rights to Samsung packages. Share original research/source with accurate tested-device scope, not proprietary APKs, bundled modules or unauthorized mirrors. Do not claim guaranteed moderator approval. Complete current Coolapk publishing rules were not verified; platform review and copyright authorization are separate requirements.
